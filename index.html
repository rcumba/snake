INTO THE UNDERGROWTH

Snake.

Eat · Grow · Survive

How to Play

Steer with arrow keys, WASD, or the direction buttons. You cannot reverse into yourself.

Eat apples for 10 points each. Your snake grows and gradually speeds up. Avoid the walls and your own body.

Every 5 normal apples gives a 40% chance of a glowing Giant Apple: +30 points and 3 growth segments. Eat any part of it within 10 seconds of active play. Normal apples remain available.

Press Space / P to pause or resume. Restart begins a new game. Leaving the window pauses play.
